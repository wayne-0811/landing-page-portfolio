Stage 02 · Weeks 5–6

Site

We walk the land with you and read it: sun, slope, views, access, water and soil. The site shapes the house more than anything else.

What happens
We walk your land together.
What you decide
You arrange access and any survey.
What it costs
Free
How long
1 week

Who you’ll meet

Your project lead and our site engineer.

You leave with

  • Site notes and photographs
  • A list of the surveys you’ll need
  • First thoughts on where the house could sit

Stage 03 · Weeks 9–20

Pre-construction

Design, materials, cost and schedule, resolved together before anything is built. Weekly workshops keep every decision in the open.

What happens
Design, cost plan and schedule.
What you decide
You agree style, budget and finishes.
What it costs
Fixed fee of $18,500, credited to your build
How long
8–12 weeks

Who you’ll meet

Your project lead, your architect and our estimator.

You leave with

  • Drawings ready for permit
  • A line-by-line cost plan
  • A week-by-week build schedule

Stage 04 · Weeks 22–23

Contract

Open-book cost-plus or one fixed price, in plain language, with the drawings, allowances and schedule attached. We walk you through every page.

What happens
Cost-plus or fixed price, in writing.
What you decide
You choose and sign.
What it costs
No fee
How long
2 weeks

Who you’ll meet

Your project lead and our contracts manager.

You leave with

  • A signed contract
  • Allowances set for finishes still to choose
  • Your login to the client portal

Stage 05 · 12–18 months

Build

A typical month on site. One project lead runs your build from first dig to final coat, and the site is always open to you.

What happens
One project lead, weekly reports.
What you decide
You approve changes in the client portal.
What it costs
Monthly draws
How long
12–18 months

Who you’ll meet

Your project lead every week, and the trades as they arrive.

You leave with

  • A weekly report with photos
  • Every invoice, open book
  • A say on every change before it happens
