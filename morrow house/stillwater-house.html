The challenges

What made this one hard.

  • Problem

    A 40-metre pool on sloping clay.

    Solution

    Helical piers and a sealed liner keep the water level and the ground still.

  • Problem

    Six-metre glass walls with no visible posts.

    Solution

    A steel frame hidden in the roof lets the glass run uninterrupted.
