The challenges

What made this one hard.

  • Problem

    A site on the edge of the creek’s flood plain.

    Solution

    The house sits on a raised plinth two feet above the 100-year flood line, and the garden is left free to flood.

  • Problem

    Three generations, three sets of needs.

    Solution

    We held design sessions with each generation and planned a quiet room for everyone around one shared kitchen.
