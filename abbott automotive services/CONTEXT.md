with it. "We'll come and get it" beats any headline about quality or experience.

contact) — not now.

2. Hero — free collection and drop-off, plus the reg-plate enquiry input.
3. Services — the five above, plain language.

7. Contact — phone, WhatsApp, hours, address.
8. Footer.

**One CTA per section.** Every CTA does the same thing: get Ollie on the phone.

## Rules

**Hard requirements**

- Phone number in the header on every page, tap-to-call
- WhatsApp link (mobile number — many customers prefer it)
- Unit number and business park name wherever the address appears
- Fast on a phone on mobile data
- Responsive to mobile, visible keyboard focus, reduced motion respected

**Hard bans**

- No stock photography — his real photos only
- No invented reviews, review counts, years trading, staff numbers or accreditations
- No unqualified "free collection" claim until the asterisk conditions are known
- No "cheapest in Northwich" or price claims of any kind
- No contact form that goes nowhere — if the enquiry input can't send yet, say so when
  you send the link
