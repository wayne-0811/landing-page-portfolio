What we do

One garage, the whole job

Servicing to sensors — in plain English, quoted before we start. If it's not something we do in-house, we'll tell you straight.

Diagnostics

Warning light on? We plug in, read the fault properly and explain what it actually means — before anyone talks about replacing parts.

Servicing & MOTs

Full and interim services, plus MOTs arranged. We'll tell you what needs doing now and what can safely wait.

Wheel alignment

Car pulling to one side or wearing tyres unevenly? We set the geometry back to where it should be.

Oil & fluid changes

Oil and filter changes, and a proper check of your brake, coolant and other fluids while it's up on the ramp.

Repairs

Brakes, clutches and the jobs a full service turns up. We diagnose it, quote it, then get it sorted.

Collection & drop-off

Can't get to us? We'll collect the car, do the work and bring it back — free within our local area.
