What we do

We explain it before we touch it

Plain English, an honest diagnosis, and a price before any spanner comes out. If you don't know what's wrong, that's fine — that's our job to work out.

Diagnostics

Warning light on? We plug in, read the fault properly, and tell you what it actually means before anything gets booked in.

Servicing & MOTs

Full and interim servicing to keep your car running right and hold its value. MOTs arranged too — ask us and we'll sort it.

Wheel alignment

Car pulling to one side, or tyres wearing on one edge? We'll set your alignment back straight so your tyres last as long as they should.

Oil & fluid changes

Fresh oil, a new filter and a proper check of your fluids and levels — the small job that stops the big one further down the road.

Clutch, brakes & repairs

Clutch slipping? Brakes not feeling right? We diagnose it, quote it, and only get started once you've said yes to the price.

Free collection & drop-off*

No lift to the garage, or stuck at work? We'll collect your car, do the work, and bring it back to you — free, locally.
